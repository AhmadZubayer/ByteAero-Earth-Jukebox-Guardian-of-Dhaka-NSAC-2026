# Earth Jukebox Guardian of Dhaka

An environmental storytelling project by **ByteAero** for the **NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026**, responding to **The Information Jukebox** challenge.

Earth Jukebox Guardian of Dhaka explores how Earth science data can become a musical story about Dhaka. The project brings together temperature, urban heat, vegetation, and rainfall data to connect environmental change with sound.

## Creative Approach

The project uses sonification—the representation of data through sound—to make environmental patterns easier to explore through listening. Vegetation greenness informs musical chords, while rainfall intensity informs drum rhythms. Historical temperature, surface heat, and future climate scenarios provide the environmental context for the story of Dhaka.
